## Why this matters
A high **Safqa recommended-retail minus supplier-reference** gap does not imply we can sell profitably at that price. Buying intent depends on the Egyptian market, product identity/specifications, COD trust, delivery policy, and advertising acquisition cost. Our supplier `sale_price` is a reference quote, NOT independently verified full purchasing cost or commission.

## Operational constraints and decision
- Real-time price and delivery of the reference offers may change, and listing prices may exclude shipping or involve different features/quality/warranties.
- The private owner dashboard's Top-20 shortlist is a **triage queue**, not a paid-ad recommendation. Market price checks and source cost/commission confirmation are still prerequisites.
- No arbitrary over/under-cutting: public store continues to show **unique verified Safqa suggested-retail note + 110 EGP inclusive delivery**, with 72 unverified prices held. Owner business approval remains separate.
- Prioritize testing products that solve a visible problem, have a verifiable video playable for anonymous customers, enough contribution after real ads/returns, and competitive all-in price.
- Proof required before ads: supplier product/variant availability, source note retail, market comparable same spec, shipping/commission/fees, return risk, landing/mobile checkout behavior and GA4/Meta event verification.
