# SELECT SHOP V15 — Guided Motion Design Plan

## Human shopping principles
1. One decision at a time: model → size → review → checkout.
2. Never make the shopper choose between two equivalent CTAs.
3. Model browsing should not throw the shopper to another part of the page unexpectedly.
4. Every state change must be visible immediately.
5. Motion should confirm actions, not delay them.
6. Mobile is the reference experience; desktop receives richer pointer effects only.
7. Try-on and purchase stay visually and financially distinct.

## Motion system
- Brand wordmark sweep on first entrance.
- Hero copy / title / price / CTA staged entrance.
- Ambient product-stage aura (subtle, slow).
- Product image blur/scale transition on model/variant switching.
- Model-card spotlight, glint and depth on desktop.
- Center-card emphasis on mobile carousel.
- Spring product sheet opening.
- Animated 3-step purchase journey.
- Variant/size selection pop feedback.
- Cart icon/count celebration after saving a selection.
- Reduced-motion support disables decorative movement.

## Guided purchase flow
- Hero primary CTA opens product selector.
- Hero secondary CTA scrolls to other models.
- Catalog CTA `اختاري الموديل` activates that product and opens its selector directly.
- Product selector shows model → size → confirmation progress.
- Secondary sheet action is `تغيير الموديل`, not a competing cart action.
- Primary sheet action confirms the choice and always opens cart review.
- Cart is where the shopper sees purchase vs try-on, extra-pair discount, total, then checkout.
